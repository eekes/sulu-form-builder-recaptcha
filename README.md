# reCAPTCHA v3 for the Sulu Form Builder

Invisible reCAPTCHA v3 as a spam check for forms built with the
[Sulu Form Builder](https://sulu.eekes.be/sulu-form-builder-bundle). Editors switch it on per form,
visitors never see a challenge, and blocked submissions land in the **Spam** tab of the form, where a
person caught by mistake can be released.

## What's inside

- [**How it decides**](https://sulu.eekes.be/sulu-form-builder-bundle/recaptcha#how-it-decides): a fresh token on every send, verified once when the visitor finishes, and never stuck when Google can't be reached.
- [**What the visitor sees**](https://sulu.eekes.be/sulu-form-builder-bundle/recaptcha#what-the-visitor-sees): nothing but Google's badge; an expired token gives the step back instead of losing the submission.
- [**The Spam tab**](https://sulu.eekes.be/sulu-form-builder-bundle/spam-protection#the-spam-tab): every blocked submission with its score and reason.
- [**Privacy and Content Security Policy**](https://sulu.eekes.be/sulu-form-builder-bundle/recaptcha#privacy-and-content-security-policy): what to add to your privacy policy and CSP.
